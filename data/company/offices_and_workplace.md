# Offices, Hybrid Working and Workplace Facilities

## Office locations

### Bengaluru (headquarters)

- Embassy Tech Village, Devarabeesanahalli, Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru 560103.
- Floors 4 to 7. Reception is on floor 4.
- Access is by Okta-provisioned badge. Badge collection is on day one from the
  Workplace desk on floor 4.
- Building hours 07:00 to 22:00 on weekdays; weekend access needs prior approval.

### Austin (Americas)

- 4th Street, Downtown Austin, Texas.
- Two floors, shared reception with the building.
- Serves the Americas commercial team and part of People & Workplace.

### Berlin (EMEA)

- Prenzlauer Berg, Berlin.
- One floor. Serves EMEA Customer Success and Sales.

Employees in countries without an office work remotely, with a co-working
allowance described in the expense policy.

## Hybrid working model

Manna's is **hybrid by default**:

- Two days per week in your home office is the baseline expectation.
- The specific days are agreed within your pod, not mandated centrally, so that
  the whole pod overlaps on at least one day.
- **Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.** Meetings involving India
  and Berlin should sit inside this window. Meetings involving Austin usually run
  early morning IST, and no one is expected to take recurring meetings before
  08:00 or after 21:00 local time.
- Fully remote arrangements are role-dependent and need approval from both your
  function lead and People & Workplace.

### Working from another country

Short trips are fine, but working from a country other than your employment
country for **more than 21 calendar days in a rolling 12 months** creates tax and
immigration exposure. Requests must be approved in advance by People & Workplace
and Finance. Some countries are excluded entirely for sanctions or tax reasons;
the current exclusion list sits with Legal.

## Accessibility

All three offices are step-free from street level to the Manna's floors.
Accessible toilets are available on every Manna's floor. If you need a workplace
adjustment, whether physical, sensory, neurological or related to a health
condition, contact People & Workplace at `#ask-people-ops`. Adjustments are
handled confidentially and are not shared with your manager without your consent
beyond what is needed to implement them.

## Visitors

All visitors must be registered in the Workplace app at least two hours ahead
and must be escorted while on the floor. Visitors sign an NDA at reception before
being admitted beyond the meeting room area. Customer visitors on tours must not
be shown screens containing another customer's data.

## Emergencies

- In Bengaluru, dial 112 for emergency services, then notify building security on
  extension 4000.
- Assembly points are posted by every stairwell; floor wardens wear orange
  lanyards during drills.
- Report any security or safety incident in `#security-incidents` as well as to
  building security.
