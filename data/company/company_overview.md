# Manna's Analytics - Company Overview

> **Note:** Manna's Analytics is a fictional company. All figures, names and
> policies in this knowledge base are synthetic and exist purely to demonstrate
> the AI Training Assistant.

## Who we are

Manna's Analytics is a B2B software company that helps mid-market and enterprise
customers turn operational data into decisions. Our platform ingests data from
warehouses, SaaS tools and event streams, then delivers governed metrics,
anomaly alerts and embedded dashboards to business teams.

We were founded in **2017** in Bengaluru by **Ananya Rao** and **Vikram Iyer**
after they spent years building internal reporting tools at a logistics company
and grew tired of every team maintaining its own conflicting version of the
same number.

## What we sell

Manna's Analytics sells a single platform with three commercial modules:

1. **Manna's Core** - the governed semantic layer and metrics catalogue. This is
   the anchor product; roughly 90% of customers start here.
2. **Manna's Signals** - automated anomaly detection and alerting on top of the
   metrics defined in Core.
3. **Manna's Embed** - white-labelled dashboards that our customers ship inside
   their own products to their own end users.

Pricing is subscription-based, billed annually, and scales with the number of
modelled metrics and monthly active viewers rather than seats.

## How we are organised

Manna's runs seven functions, each led by a member of the executive team:
Engineering, Data & AI, Product & Design, Sales, Customer Success, People &
Workplace, and Finance & Legal. Detailed team charters live in the role and
team documents.

## Working model

We are **hybrid by default**. Most employees are expected in their home office
two days per week, with the specific days agreed inside each team rather than
mandated centrally. Fully remote arrangements exist but are role-dependent and
must be approved by both the function lead and People & Workplace.

Core collaboration hours are **11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time**, which gives
a workable overlap with Berlin and an early-morning window with Austin.

## The onboarding journey at a glance

New joiners follow a structured 90-day ramp:

- **Days 1-5:** Orientation, systems access, security training, meet your buddy.
- **Days 6-30:** Function-specific training, shadowing, first small deliverable.
- **Days 31-60:** Owning a scoped piece of work with review support.
- **Days 61-90:** Operating at expected level for your grade, 90-day review.

The full checklist, including who is accountable for each step, is maintained in
the onboarding documentation owned by People & Workplace.
