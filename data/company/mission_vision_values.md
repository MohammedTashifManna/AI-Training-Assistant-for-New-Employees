# Mission, Vision and Values

## Our mission

**Make every operational decision traceable to a trusted number.**

We exist because most companies do not lack data, they lack agreement about
what the data means. Our job is to remove that ambiguity.

## Our vision

By 2030 we want "what does Manna's say?" to be the normal way a mid-market
operations team settles a disagreement about performance, the same way teams
today reach for a version control system to settle a disagreement about code.

## Our five values

### 1. One number, one meaning

We do not ship features that let two teams define the same metric two ways
without knowing it. Internally, this means we push disagreements about
definitions to the surface early rather than papering over them.

**In practice:** if you find two conflicting numbers in a document or a
dashboard, raising it is always welcome and never treated as nitpicking.

### 2. Show the working

Conclusions travel with their evidence. A recommendation without its
supporting query, sample or source is treated as an opinion, not a finding.

**In practice:** link to the source in your write-ups. "Trust me" is not a
supported citation format.

### 3. Customer-close, not customer-captive

We spend real time with customers and we take their problems seriously, but we
do not build a bespoke product for every request. We look for the general shape
behind a specific ask.

**In practice:** feature requests go through Product with the underlying job to
be done described, not just the requested widget.

### 4. Short feedback, low drama

We would rather have an awkward conversation this week than a resentful one
next quarter. Feedback is expected in both directions, including upward.

**In practice:** disagreement is normal in design reviews. Personal attacks are
not, and are handled under the Code of Conduct.

### 5. Leave the campsite tidier

Whoever touches a system last is responsible for leaving it a little better
documented than they found it. This applies to code, dashboards, runbooks and
onboarding docs.

**In practice:** if onboarding documentation confused you, fixing it is a
legitimate use of your first-month time and is explicitly encouraged.

## How values show up in performance reviews

Values are assessed alongside delivery in every review cycle. Consistently
strong delivery paired with repeated values concerns will not result in a strong
overall rating. The career framework describes the specific behavioural
indicators used at each grade.

## Rituals that reinforce the values

| Ritual | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Hands | Monthly, first Thursday | Company metrics, roadmap, Q&A with the exec team |
| Metric Review | Weekly, Tuesdays | Function leads walk through their own numbers in Manna's |
| Demo Friday | Fortnightly | Anyone can demo anything, including unfinished work |
| Retro Roulette | Quarterly | Cross-function retrospective on a recent launch |
| Onboarding Circle | Monthly | New joiners from the last month meet the exec team |

Attendance at All Hands is expected; recordings and notes are posted for those
in incompatible time zones.
