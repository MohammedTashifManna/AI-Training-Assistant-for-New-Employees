# Glossary and Internal Acronyms

New joiners consistently report that internal shorthand is the single most
confusing part of their first fortnight. This page is the canonical list.

## Product vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Core** | Manna's Core, the governed semantic layer and metrics catalogue |
| **Signals** | Manna's Signals, the anomaly detection and alerting module |
| **Embed** | Manna's Embed, white-labelled dashboards shipped inside customer products |
| **Metric spec** | The version-controlled YAML definition of a single metric |
| **Semantic layer** | The mapping between raw warehouse tables and business metrics |
| **Lineage** | The dependency graph from a metric back to its source columns |
| **Freshness SLA** | The promised maximum age of the data behind a metric |
| **Catalogue** | The browsable index of all metrics in a customer's account |
| **Tenant** | One isolated customer account; Embed enforces per-tenant security |
| **Connector** | An integration that pulls data from a source system |
| **Backfill** | Recomputing historical values after a metric definition changes |
| **Drift** | A metric slowly diverging from its documented definition |

## Company and process vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pod** | Cross-functional delivery team of five to nine people |
| **Group** | Durable ownership area within a function; owns systems and budget |
| **Function** | Your discipline, for example Engineering or Customer Success |
| **Functional manager** | Owns your growth, review and compensation |
| **Pod lead** | Owns your day-to-day priorities |
| **Buddy** | An experienced peer assigned to you for your first 90 days |
| **Ramp** | The structured 90-day onboarding journey |
| **Metric Review** | Weekly Tuesday session where leads present their own numbers |
| **Demo Friday** | Fortnightly open demo session, unfinished work welcome |
| **Onboarding Circle** | Monthly session where the month's new joiners meet the exec team |
| **Founders' Review** | Monthly forum to bring a hard trade-off to the founders |
| **Retro Roulette** | Quarterly cross-function retrospective |

## Phrases that confuse newcomers

- **"Is that a Core question or a Signals question?"** - asking which module the
  customer issue belongs to, so it reaches the right pod.
- **"Show the working."** - a request for the evidence behind your conclusion,
  drawn from our second company value.
- **"That's drifted."** - a metric no longer matches its documented definition.
- **"Take it to AR."** - bring the design to Architecture Review.
- **"Campsite it."** - tidy up the documentation while you are in there, from our
  fifth value.
