# Product, Design, People and Finance - Role Guide

## Product & Design

**Function lead:** Priya Menon, Chief Product Officer
**Headcount:** 70

### What the function owns

Product decides what gets built and why; Design decides how it feels to use;
Engineering decides how it is built. Product does not assign work to engineers,
it sets the outcome and the constraints.

### Roles

| Role | Grade band | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Associate Product Manager | L3 | A feature area with close mentoring |
| Product Manager | L4-L5 | A pod's outcomes, discovery and roadmap |
| Senior / Group Product Manager | L5-L6 | A module or several pods |
| Director of Product | L7 | A product line and its commercial performance |
| Product Designer | L3-L6 | End-to-end design for a pod |
| Design Systems Engineer | L4-L6 | The shared component library |
| User Researcher | L4-L6 | Study design, interview programme, synthesis |
| Technical Writer | L3-L5 | Product documentation and in-product copy |

### How work gets decided

- Quarterly planning sets outcomes, not feature lists.
- Every initiative needs a one-page brief: problem, evidence, target outcome,
  measurable success criteria, and what we are explicitly not doing.
- **Product Council** meets fortnightly to sequence work across modules.
- Discovery expectation: at least five customer conversations before committing
  to a significant build.
- Designs go through a weekly design critique. Engineering attends.

### Rules

- Roadmap dates are **never** communicated externally without CPO sign-off.
- Feature requests from customers are logged with the underlying job to be done,
  not just the requested widget. This comes straight from our third value.

**Function lead:** Sarah Whitfield, Chief People Officer

| Team | Owns |
| --- | --- |

Analyst, L&D Programme Manager, Workplace Coordinator, People Operations
Specialist.

### What to contact whom about

| Topic | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Payslip, tax declaration, employment letter | People Operations, `#ask-people-ops` |
| Conflict with a colleague or manager | Your People Partner |
| Training budget or a course request | Learning & Development |
| Desk, badge, office facilities, business travel booking | Workplace |
| Compensation review question | Your manager first, then Total Rewards |

## Cross-cutting note for every function

Whatever your function, two things are true from day one:

1. You have a **functional manager** who owns your growth and a **pod lead** who
   owns your priorities. If they conflict, your functional manager arbitrates.
2. You are expected to complete security and data-handling training in your first
   week. Several systems will not be provisioned until it is done.
