# Career Framework, Grades and Performance

## Grade ladder

Manna's uses a single grade ladder across every function, so an L5 in Engineering
and an L5 in Customer Success sit at comparable scope and comparable pay bands.

| Grade | Individual contributor title pattern | Typical scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L2 | Associate / Intern-converted | Learning the craft, closely supervised |
| L3 | Engineer / Analyst / Associate PM | Delivers well-defined tasks reliably |
| L4 | Engineer II / Analyst II / PM | Owns a feature or workstream end to end |
| L5 | Senior | Owns a subsystem or area; mentors others |
| L6 | Staff / Group | Impact across several pods |
| L7 | Principal / Director | Sets direction for a domain or product line |
| L8 | Distinguished / VP | Company-level technical or functional direction |

The management track runs in parallel: **M1** (team lead, roughly L5 scope),
**M2** (manager of a group, roughly L6), **M3** (senior manager or director,
roughly L7). Moving between IC and management tracks in either direction is
treated as a lateral move, not a promotion or demotion, and does not change your
grade or pay band.

## What is assessed

Every review looks at two axes, weighted equally:

1. **Delivery** - the scope, quality and reliability of your output against the
   expectations of your grade.
2. **Values and behaviours** - how you worked, assessed against the five company
   values.

Strong delivery paired with repeated values concerns will not produce a strong
overall rating. This is deliberate and is stated in the values documentation.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional | Consistently operating a full grade above expectations |
| Exceeds | Delivered clearly beyond the expectations of the grade |
| Meets | Delivered what the grade expects; this is a good, healthy rating |
| Developing | Below expectation in some areas; a written development plan follows |
| Not meeting | Sustained shortfall; a formal performance improvement plan follows |

Roughly two thirds of employees land on **Meets** in a normal cycle. "Meets" is
not a warning sign, and managers are coached to say so explicitly.

## Review cycle

The fiscal year runs 1 April to 31 March, and reviews follow it.

| Activity | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Goal setting | April |
| Mid-year check-in (no rating) | October |
| Self-assessment | Late February |
| Peer feedback (3-5 reviewers) | Late February |
| Manager assessment and calibration | March |
| Ratings and compensation communicated | Mid April |
| Promotions effective | 1 May |

### If you joined mid-year

You need **at least three months of tenure** by 31 March to receive a rating. If
you joined later than that, you take part in goal setting and the check-in but
receive your first rating in the following cycle. Your first-year compensation
review is pro-rated by months of service.

## Promotion

Promotion is a recognition that you are **already** operating at the next grade
consistently, not a reward for tenure or a bet on potential.

- Nominated by your functional manager, evidenced by a written case with examples.
- Calibrated across the function so the bar is consistent between managers.
- L6 and above additionally reviewed by the function's executive sponsor.
- Typical time in grade before promotion: 18 to 30 months at L3-L5, longer above.

## The 90-day review for new joiners

Separate from the annual cycle, every new joiner has a 90-day review:

- **Day 30:** informal check-in with your manager, plus a separate one with your
  People Partner.
- **Day 60:** written progress note from your manager, no rating.
- **Day 90:** formal confirmation conversation covering what is going well, what
  needs to change, and confirmation of successful completion of probation.

Probation in India is **six months** from your start date; confirmation is
normally granted at the 90-day mark if the review is positive, with the remaining
period used for continued ramp support. Notice during probation is 30 days on
either side.

## Compensation structure

| Component | Applies to | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base salary | Everyone | Reviewed annually, effective 1 May |
| Performance bonus | Non-quota roles | Target 10-20% of base by grade, paid annually in May |
| Sales variable | Quota-carrying roles | Paid quarterly after collections |
| Equity (RSUs) | L4 and above | Four-year vest, one-year cliff, quarterly thereafter |
| On-call allowance | Engineers in a rotation | Weekly allowance plus time off in lieu |

Pay bands per grade and location are published internally on the Total Rewards
page. Manna's benchmarks to the **65th percentile** of its comparator set.

## Growth support

- **Learning budget:** INR 60,000 per employee per financial year (or local
  equivalent) for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Approved by your
  manager, claimed through the expense system under "Learning & Development".
- **Internal mobility:** you may apply for internal roles after **12 months** in
  your current role. Your manager is informed once you reach the interview stage,
  not before you apply.
- **Mentoring:** the L&D team runs a matching programme each quarter.
- **Conference speaking:** supported and funded; loop in Corporate Communications
  before submitting a talk that references Manna's systems or customers.
