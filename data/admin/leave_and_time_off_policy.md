# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Policy version 5.0, effective 1 April 2026.** Leave year runs 1 April to
31 March, matching the fiscal year. All leave is requested and approved in
**Workday**.

## Entitlements at a glance (India)

| Leave type | Entitlement per leave year | Carry-forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privilege (annual) leave | 22 working days | Up to 10 days |
| Casual / sick leave | 12 days | None |
| Public holidays | 12 days (10 fixed + 2 floating) | None |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks paid | Not applicable |
| Paternity / secondary carer leave | 4 weeks paid | Not applicable |
| Adoption / surrogacy leave | 12 weeks paid primary carer | Not applicable |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days per instance | Not applicable |
| Marriage leave | 5 days, once during employment | Not applicable |
| Compassionate / carer leave | 5 days | None |
| Unpaid leave (LOP) | Case by case | Not applicable |

Employees in the USA and Germany follow local schedules published on the People
& Workplace page; entitlements there are at least equal to statutory minimums and
the parental provisions are matched globally.

## Accrual and joining part-way through the year

- Privilege leave accrues at **1.83 days per completed month** of service.
- If you join mid-year your entitlement is pro-rated by completed months.
- You may take accrued leave from **day one**; there is no waiting period. You
  cannot go into a negative balance without People Partner approval.
- Casual/sick leave is credited in full at the start of the leave year (pro-rated
  in your joining year) and does not accrue monthly.

## Notice and approval

| Duration of absence | Notice expected |
| --- | --- |
| 1-2 days | 3 working days ahead |
| 3-5 days | 1 week ahead |
| 6-10 days | 3 weeks ahead |
| More than 10 days | 6 weeks ahead |

Sick leave is exempt from notice requirements: inform your manager as early as
you reasonably can on the day.

Managers approve or decline in Workday within **3 working days**. A decline must
carry a written reason and, where the reason is a delivery conflict, a proposed
alternative window.

## Carry-forward, encashment and expiry

- Up to **10 days** of unused privilege leave carry into the next leave year.
- Carried-forward days must be used by **30 September** or they lapse.
- Encashment is not available during employment. On exit, unused privilege leave
  up to the carry-forward cap is paid out with the final settlement.
- Casual/sick leave never carries forward and is never encashed.

## Public holidays

Ten fixed holidays are published each December for the following calendar year.
Two **floating holidays** let you observe festivals or occasions that matter to
you and are booked in Workday like any other leave, with 3 working days notice.

Holiday calendars differ by office. When working with colleagues in another
location, check their calendar before scheduling.

## Sick leave and medical certificates

- Up to 2 consecutive days: no certificate required.
- 3 or more consecutive days: a medical certificate is required, submitted to
  People Operations, not to your manager.
- Extended illness beyond your sick leave balance moves to privilege leave and
  then, if needed, to a paid medical leave arrangement agreed with your People
  Partner. Nobody is expected to exhaust all leave and then go unpaid without a
  conversation first.

## Parental leave

### Maternity

26 weeks fully paid, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Can start up to 8
weeks before the expected date. A phased return over 4 weeks at reduced hours on
full pay is available on request.

### Paternity and secondary carer

4 weeks fully paid, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or placement. May be
taken in up to three separate blocks.

### Shared provisions

- Applies equally regardless of gender, marital status or how the family was
  formed, including adoption and surrogacy.
- Your role is held for you. Return-to-work conversations start 4 weeks before
  your planned return.
- Statutory minimums always apply where they are more generous than this policy.

## Other leave

**Bereavement:** 5 days per instance for an immediate family member, extendable by
agreement with your People Partner. No documentation is requested.

**Carer leave:** 5 days per leave year to care for a dependent.

**Jury duty, voting and statutory obligations:** granted as paid leave for the
required duration.

**Sabbatical:** after **4 years** of continuous service, up to 8 weeks unpaid with
your role held. Requires function head approval and 3 months notice.

**On-call TOIL:** engineers paged outside working hours claim time off in lieu in
Workday under "On-call TOIL". It must be used within 60 days.

## Interaction with notice periods

Leave cannot normally be taken during your notice period on exit, other than
already-approved leave, and is not used to shorten notice. Any exception needs
People Partner agreement.

## Common questions

**Can I take leave during probation?** Yes. Accrued leave is available from day
one.

**Do weekends and public holidays inside a leave period count against my
balance?** No. Only working days are deducted.

**What if my manager has not responded?** After 3 working days, escalate to your
group lead or `#ask-people-ops`.

**Can I work while on leave?** No. Leave means being off. If a genuine emergency
pulls you back in, cancel the affected days in Workday so your balance is restored.

**Who covers my work?** Agree cover with your pod before requesting anything
longer than 5 days, and note the cover arrangement in the request.

## Contact

`#ask-people-ops` for entitlement and balance questions. Your People Partner for
anything sensitive, including medical or family circumstances.
