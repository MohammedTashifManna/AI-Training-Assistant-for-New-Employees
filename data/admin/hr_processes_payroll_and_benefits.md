# HR Processes, Payroll and Benefits

Unless stated otherwise, this document describes the **India entity** (Manna's
Analytics Technologies Private Limited). USA and Germany schedules are published
separately on the People & Workplace page.

## Payroll

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Pay frequency | Monthly |
| Pay date | Last **working** day of the month |
| Cut-off for changes | 18th of the month |
| Payslips | Workday, from the 1st of the following month |
| Form 16 | Issued by 15 June for the previous financial year |
| Currency | INR for India, USD for USA, EUR for Germany |

If your pay date falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the
preceding working day.

### Salary structure (India)

| Component | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | Typically 40% of fixed CTC; drives PF and gratuity |
| House Rent Allowance | 50% of basic for metro locations |
| Special allowance | Balancing component |
| Employer PF contribution | 12% of basic, statutory |
| Gratuity provision | Payable after 5 years of continuous service |
| Flexible benefit plan | Declare in April, see below |
| Performance bonus or sales variable | Per your offer letter |

### Flexible benefit plan

Declare allocations in Workday during **April** each year, or within 30 days of
joining. Components include telephone and internet reimbursement, fuel and driver
allowance, books and periodicals, professional development, and Leave Travel
Allowance. Bills must be submitted by **15 January** for the tax year; unclaimed
amounts are paid out as taxable salary.

### Tax declaration

- Declare your regime choice (old or new) and planned investments in April.
- Proof submission window: **1 December to 15 January**.
- Missing the proof deadline means higher tax deducted in February and March; you
  can still claim in your own return.
- People Operations cannot give personal tax advice. A tax helpdesk session with an
  external advisor is run each January.

## Benefits

### Health insurance

| Cover | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Group medical, cashless network |
| Sum insured | INR 800,000 family floater |
| Covered | You, spouse or domestic partner, up to 2 children, and up to 2 parents or parents-in-law |
| Pre-existing conditions | Covered from day one, no waiting period |
| Maternity sub-limit | INR 150,000, no waiting period |
| Premium | Employee, spouse and children fully funded by Manna's; parents partially employee-funded |

**Enrol dependents within 30 days of joining.** After that you must wait for the
annual enrolment window in April, other than for a qualifying life event
(marriage, birth, adoption), which reopens enrolment for 30 days.

### Other insurance

- **Group term life:** 3x annual fixed salary, employer funded.
- **Group personal accident:** 3x annual fixed salary, employer funded.
- **Top-up medical cover:** optional, employee funded, elected in April.

### Wellbeing

- **Employee Assistance Programme:** 12 free confidential counselling sessions per
  year for you and your household, run by an external provider. Manna's receives
  aggregate usage numbers only and never individual names.
- **Annual health check:** fully funded, once per financial year.
- **Wellness allowance:** INR 2,000 per month, claimed quarterly through Expensify.
- **Mental health days:** taken as casual/sick leave, no explanation required.

### Family and other benefits

- **Creche reimbursement:** up to INR 8,000 per month per child under 6.
- **Relocation support:** for role-mandated relocation, per the relocation policy.
- **Referral bonus:** INR 75,000 for a successful referral, paid after the referred
  employee completes 90 days.
- **Higher education support:** partial funding for approved part-time programmes,
  with a service commitment; assessed case by case by L&D.

## Working hours and attendance

- Standard week: 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- **Core collaboration hours 11:00-16:00 IST**; hours outside that are flexible by
  arrangement with your pod.
- No formal clock-in. Attendance is managed through delivery and through leave
  records in Workday.
- Hybrid expectation is two days per week in your home office, with the days agreed
  inside your pod.

## Internal moves and exits

### Internal transfers

Eligible after **12 months** in your current role. Apply through the internal
careers page in Workday. Your current manager is informed when you reach the
interview stage, not when you apply. Standard transition notice between teams is
30 days.

### Resignation and notice

| Situation | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| During probation | 30 days, either side |
| Confirmed employee, L3-L5 | 60 days |
| Confirmed employee, L6 and above | 90 days |

Notice is served in Workday. Buy-out of notice is at the discretion of your
function head and the CFO. Leave cannot normally be used to shorten notice.

### Exit process

1. Resignation submitted and acknowledged in Workday.
2. Handover document agreed with your manager within the first week of notice.
3. Exit interview with your People Partner.
4. Clearance from IT, Finance, Workplace and Legal.
5. Return all company equipment on or before your last working day.
6. **Full and final settlement within 45 days** of your last working day, including
   payout of unused privilege leave up to the carry-forward cap.
7. Relieving letter and experience certificate issued with the settlement.
8. Gratuity paid if you have completed 5 years of continuous service.

Access is revoked within 4 hours of your last working day. Confidentiality and IP
obligations continue after employment ends.

## Escalation

Start with `#ask-people-ops` for process questions. For anything sensitive,
personal, or involving your manager, contact your **People Partner** directly. For
conduct, harassment or ethics concerns, use the routes in the Code of Conduct,
which are designed to bypass your management chain.
