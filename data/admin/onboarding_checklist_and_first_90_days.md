# Onboarding Checklist and First 90 Days

This is the canonical onboarding checklist. Every item names who is accountable,
so nothing sits in the gap between "someone should" and "nobody did".

## Before you start (pre-boarding)

| # | Item | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Signed offer and background verification cleared | Talent Acquisition |
| 2 | Documents submitted: ID proof, PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, prior employment relieving letter, education certificates, bank details | You |
| 3 | Laptop shipped or reserved for collection | IT |
| 4 | Okta account created, welcome email sent to personal address | IT |
| 5 | Buddy assigned and introduced by email | Your manager |
| 6 | First-week calendar populated | Your manager |
| 7 | Payroll and statutory records created (PF, ESI where applicable, gratuity) | People Operations |

If you have not received your welcome email **3 working days** before your start
date, contact Talent Acquisition. Do not wait until day one.

## Day 1

| # | Item | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Arrive 09:30 IST at floor 4 reception, or join the remote orientation link | You |
| 2 | Collect badge and laptop; badge photo taken at the Workplace desk | Workplace / IT |
| 3 | Orientation session: company overview, values, how we work (2 hours) | L&D |
| 4 | Set up Okta MFA, 1Password, Slack, Google Workspace, Workday | You, with IT support |
| 5 | Acknowledge the Code of Conduct in Workday | You |
| 6 | Start **Security Essentials** training | You |
| 7 | Meet your manager: role expectations, first-week plan | Your manager |
| 8 | Meet your buddy: office tour, informal Q&A, lunch | Your buddy |
| 9 | Join your function, group and pod Slack channels | You |
| 10 | Verify your personal details and bank account in Workday | You |

## Week 1 (days 2-5)

| # | Item | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Complete **Security Essentials** and **Data Handling & Privacy** training | You |
| 2 | Function-specific tooling access provisioned (blocked until training is done) | IT / your manager |
| 3 | Read: company overview, mission and values, glossary, your function's role guide | You |
| 4 | Read: expense policy, leave policy, code of conduct, compliance policy | You |
| 5 | Meet each member of your pod 1:1 (30 minutes each) | You |
| 6 | Attend your pod's standup, planning and retro at least once | You |
| 7 | Confirm benefits enrolment and nominate dependents for insurance | You |
| 8 | Submit tax declaration for the financial year | You |
| 9 | Agree your 30-60-90 day plan in writing with your manager | You + manager |
| 10 | First small deliverable identified | Your manager |

**Deadline note:** insurance dependent nomination must be completed within **30
days** of joining, otherwise you wait for the next annual enrolment window.

## Days 6-30

- Complete function-specific training (secure development, or demo certification
  for customer-facing roles).
- Ship your first small deliverable end to end, with your buddy pairing.
- Attend one All Hands and one Metric Review as an observer.
- Attend the monthly **Onboarding Circle** with the exec team.
- **Day 30:** informal check-in with your manager, and a separate one with your
  People Partner. This is a two-way conversation; come with friction points.

## Days 31-60

- Own a scoped piece of work with review support rather than pairing.
- Engineering: shadow two on-call rotations.
- Data & AI: reproduce two exec-reviewed metrics and compare against Core.
- Sales / CS: run a live customer call with your manager observing.
- Give feedback on the onboarding experience itself through the L&D survey.
- **Day 60:** written progress note from your manager. No rating attached.

## Days 61-90

- Operate at the expected level for your grade with normal supervision.
- Present something you have learned or built to your team.
- Fix at least one piece of documentation that confused you. This is expected, not
  optional, and comes from our fifth value.
- **Day 90:** formal confirmation conversation with your manager covering what is
  going well, what needs to change, and confirmation of probation completion.

Probation in India is **6 months**; confirmation is normally granted at the 90-day
mark when the review is positive, with the remaining period used for continued ramp
support. Notice during probation is 30 days either side.

## Who does what during your onboarding

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | Training, reading, asking questions early, flagging blockers |
| **Your manager** | Role clarity, 30-60-90 plan, check-ins, first deliverable, day-90 review |
| **Your buddy** | Informal guidance, cultural context, "is this normal?" questions |
| **Talent Acquisition** | Pre-boarding, documents, background verification |
| **People Operations** | Contract, payroll, benefits, records |
| **IT** | Laptop, accounts, access |
| **L&D** | Orientation, training catalogue, onboarding programme quality |
| **People Partner** | Independent check-in, anything you would rather not raise with your manager |

## If something is missing

| Problem | Action |
| --- | --- |
| No laptop or account on day 1 | `#ask-it`, and tell your manager |
| No buddy assigned | Ask your manager; escalate to L&D if unresolved in 2 days |
| Access blocked and you cannot tell why | `#ask-it`; usually outstanding security training |
| Payroll or benefits not set up | `#ask-people-ops` |
| No 30-60-90 plan by end of week 1 | Raise with your manager, then your People Partner |
| Manager unavailable in your first week | Contact your People Partner |

## First-week reading list

1. Company Overview
2. Mission, Vision and Values
3. Glossary and Internal Acronyms
4. Your function's role guide
5. Expense and Reimbursement Policy
6. Leave and Time Off Policy
7. Code of Conduct
8. Compliance and Information Security Policy
9. Internal Tools and Access Guide
10. Career Framework, Grades and Performance

You are not expected to memorise these. You are expected to know they exist and
roughly what is in each, so you can find the answer later, or ask this assistant.
