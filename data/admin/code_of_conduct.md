# Code of Conduct

**Policy version 3.1, effective 1 April 2026.** Applies to every employee,
contractor, intern and board member, in every location, and at any event where
you are identifiably associated with Manna's Analytics.

Every employee acknowledges this Code during onboarding and re-acknowledges it
annually.

## 1. Treat people well

We expect professional, respectful behaviour. The following are prohibited:

- **Harassment**, including unwanted physical contact, sexual advances, persistent
  unwelcome attention, slurs, or displaying offensive material.
- **Discrimination** on the basis of gender, gender identity or expression, sexual
  orientation, caste, religion, race, ethnicity, national origin, age, disability,
  marital or parental status, or any other protected characteristic.
- **Bullying**, including sustained belittling, deliberate exclusion, public
  humiliation and abusive language.
- **Retaliation** against anyone who raises a concern or takes part in an
  investigation. Retaliation is treated as seriously as the original conduct.

Robust disagreement about work is welcome and expected. Disagreement directed at
a person rather than an idea is not.

### India-specific: prevention of sexual harassment

Manna's complies with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention,
Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. An **Internal Committee (IC)** is
constituted with an external member. Complaints may be made directly to the IC at
`ic-committee@mannasanalytics.example`, bypassing any manager. The IC acknowledges
within 3 working days and completes inquiry within 90 days.

## 2. Avoid conflicts of interest

Declare anything that could reasonably look like a conflict. Disclosure is almost
always sufficient; concealment is the problem.

Declarable situations include:

- Outside employment, consulting or advisory work, paid or unpaid.
- A financial interest in a customer, competitor, supplier or prospect.
- A close personal or family relationship with someone at a customer, competitor
  or supplier, or with a colleague where one of you influences the other's pay,
  review, promotion or hiring.
- Serving on an external board.
- Hiring or managing a relative or partner.

Declare through the Conflicts Register in Workday within **14 days** of the
situation arising. Reporting relationships between people in a personal
relationship are not permitted; People & Workplace will arrange a reporting change.

### Outside work

Side projects are allowed provided they do not compete with Manna's, do not use
Manna's time, systems, data or IP, and do not create an obligation that conflicts
with your role. Anything in the analytics, BI or semantic-layer space needs
written approval from your function head and Legal.

## 3. Protect what is confidential

- Company confidential information includes financials, roadmaps, pricing,
  customer lists, source code, security details and personnel data.
- **Customer data is not ours.** Access it only when a specific task requires it,
  only through approved systems, and never copy it to a personal device or account.
- Do not discuss non-public information in public places, on public transport, or
  on social media.
- Confidentiality obligations continue **after** your employment ends.
- Never share one customer's data, configuration or dashboards with another.

## 4. Insider dealing and non-public information

Manna's is privately held, so there is no Manna's share to trade. However you may
learn non-public information about **listed customers, partners or acquisition
targets**. Trading on that information, or passing it to anyone else, is illegal.
If you are unsure, do not trade and speak to Legal.

## 5. No bribery or improper payments

- Never offer, give, request or accept anything of value to improperly influence a
  decision. This includes facilitation payments, however small and however
  customary they are claimed to be.
- Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent, transparent and never given
  around a live tender decision. Value limits are in the expense policy.
- **Any gift or hospitality of any value to a government official or public-sector
  employee requires prior written Legal approval.**
- The same rules bind our resellers and partners; do not ask a partner to do what
  you may not do yourself.

## 6. Compete fairly

- Do not use a competitor's confidential information, and do not ask candidates or
  new joiners for it. If someone offers it, decline and tell Legal.
- Never agree with a competitor on pricing, territories, customers or wages. These
  arrangements are criminal in most of our markets.
- Describe competitors accurately. No knowingly false or misleading claims.

## 7. Use company systems responsibly

- Company systems are for company work. Limited, reasonable personal use is fine.
- No unlawful, harassing or explicit material on company systems.
- Do not install unapproved software or route company data through unapproved
  services, including AI tools. See the compliance and infosec policy for the
  approved list.
- Manna's may access company systems and data where there is a legitimate business
  or legal reason, subject to local law and the privacy notice.

## 8. Keep honest records

Expense claims, timesheets, contracts, revenue records and customer commitments
must be accurate. Never create a document intended to mislead, and never
back-date. Records under legal hold must not be deleted.

## 9. Speak up

If something looks wrong, raise it. You will not be penalised for raising a
concern in good faith, even if it turns out to be unfounded.

| Route | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| Your manager | Most day-to-day concerns |
| Your People Partner | Interpersonal issues, conduct concerns |
| Internal Committee (`ic-committee@mannasanalytics.example`) | Sexual harassment |
| `#security-incidents` and `security@mannasanalytics.example` | Security and data incidents |
| Legal (`#ask-legal`) | Bribery, conflicts, competition law, insider information |
| Ethics Line (`ethics.mannasanalytics.example`) | Anything, anonymously, 24x7, run by an independent third party |

The Ethics Line is available in English, Hindi, Kannada and German and does not
capture identifying metadata. Reports go to the General Counsel and, where the
report concerns the General Counsel or an exec team member, directly to the Audit
Committee of the board.

## 10. Consequences

Breaches are investigated proportionately and confidentially. Outcomes range from
coaching and a written warning to termination, removal of variable pay, and
referral to authorities where the law has been broken. Cooperation with an
investigation is expected; deliberately misleading an investigation is itself a
serious breach.
