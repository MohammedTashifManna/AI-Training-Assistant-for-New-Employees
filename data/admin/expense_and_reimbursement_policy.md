# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

**Policy version 4.2, effective 1 April 2026.** Supersedes version 4.1.

## Guiding principle

Spend Manna's money as if it were your own, and be able to explain any claim to a
colleague without embarrassment. Where this policy is silent, ask `#ask-finance`
before you spend, not after.

## Who approves what

| Claim value (INR) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10,000 | Auto-approved if policy-compliant and receipted |
| 10,001 - 50,000 | Your functional manager |
| 50,001 - 200,000 | Group lead or function head |
| Above 200,000 | Function head plus CFO |

USD and EUR equivalents are converted at the month's posted rate. Approval
thresholds are based on the **total claim**, not per line item. Splitting a single
purchase across claims to stay under a threshold is a policy breach.

## How to claim

1. Submit in **Expensify** within **30 days** of incurring the cost.
2. Attach an itemised receipt for every line above INR 500. Card statements are
   not receipts.
3. Select the correct category and cost centre. Your cost centre is on your
   Workday profile.
4. For any client-related spend, name the customer and the business purpose in the
   description field.

**Claims older than 60 days are rejected** except with written CFO approval.
Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run for claims approved by the 25th
of the month.

## Travel

### Booking

All business travel is booked through **TravelPerk**. Self-booked travel is
reimbursable only when TravelPerk was unavailable and you noted this in the claim.

Travel must be approved in advance by your manager. International travel
additionally needs function head approval.

### Air travel

| Flight duration | Class |
| --- | --- |
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| Any duration, L7 and above | Business |

Book at least **14 days** ahead where possible. Choosing a more expensive flight
for schedule convenience needs a one-line justification.

### Accommodation

Nightly caps, excluding taxes:

| Location | Cap per night |
| --- | --- |
| Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai | INR 7,500 |
| Mumbai, Delhi NCR | INR 9,000 |
| Other India | INR 6,000 |
| USA, UK, Western Europe, Singapore, Japan, Australia | USD 250 |
| Rest of world | USD 180 |

Exceeding a cap because of a conference venue or a customer-arranged hotel is
acceptable with prior manager approval noted on the claim.

### Ground transport

- Economy ride-hailing or metered taxi for city travel; premium tiers are not
  reimbursable except late at night or with luggage.
- Rail travel: AC 2-tier or equivalent; first class for journeys over 8 hours.
- Personal car mileage: **INR 14 per kilometre** in India, **USD 0.62 per mile**
  in the USA. Log start and end points.
- Airport parking reimbursable up to 7 days.

### Meals while travelling

Per diem, no receipts required:

| Location | Per diem |
| --- | --- |
| India | INR 2,000 per day |
| USA, UK, Western Europe, Singapore, Japan, Australia | USD 70 per day |
| Rest of world | USD 50 per day |

Per diem is not payable for meals already provided by a conference, a customer or
the company. You may claim **either** per diem **or** itemised meal receipts for a
given day, never both.

## Customer entertainment

- Reimbursable with a clear business purpose and the attendee list recorded.
- Cap of **INR 4,000 per person** in India, **USD 90 per person** elsewhere.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a customer or team meal, never as a solo
  claim, and never charged to a government or public-sector customer engagement.
- Gifts to customers must not exceed **INR 5,000** in value and must be logged.
  Anything above that, or any gift to a public official of any value, requires
  Legal approval under the anti-bribery provisions of the compliance policy.

## Home office and equipment

| Item | Entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| One-time home office setup | INR 25,000, claimable after 30 days of service |
| Monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset | Ordered through IT, not claimed as expense |
| Internet allowance | INR 1,500 per month, auto-paid with payroll, no claim needed |
| Mobile allowance | INR 1,000 per month for roles with customer contact |
| Co-working space (no local office) | Up to INR 12,000 per month with manager approval |
| Ergonomic assessment and equipment | Fully funded via People & Workplace, outside the setup cap |

Equipment bought with company funds remains Manna's property and is returned on
exit, apart from the home office setup allowance items, which are yours after two
years of service.

## Learning and development

- **INR 60,000 per financial year** for courses, books, certifications and
  conference tickets.
- Claim under category "Learning & Development" with manager approval.
- Conference travel is charged to the travel budget, not the learning budget.
- The allowance does not carry over to the next financial year.

## Wellness

INR 2,000 per month towards gym, fitness classes, sports facilities or licensed
mental-health support. Claimed quarterly. Receipts required. Equipment purchases
(bicycles, treadmills) are not covered.

## Not reimbursable

- Traffic fines, parking tickets and late-payment penalties.
- Personal travel attached to a business trip, including extra hotel nights not
  required by the itinerary.
- Travel or hotel upgrades bought with personal loyalty points and then claimed.
- In-flight entertainment, minibar, spa and laundry (laundry is reimbursable on
  trips over 6 nights).
- Alcohol claimed alone or without a business purpose.
- Software subscriptions bought personally. All software goes through Procurement,
  which is a security requirement, not just a cost control.
- Cash payments without a receipt.

## Getting it wrong

Honest mistakes are fixed by resubmitting the claim; nobody is penalised for
misreading a cap. Deliberately falsified claims are treated as fraud under the
Code of Conduct and can result in termination and recovery of funds.

## Questions

`#ask-finance` for policy and claim status. For a rejected claim, the rejection
note in Expensify names the specific rule; if you disagree, reply in the claim
thread rather than resubmitting unchanged.
